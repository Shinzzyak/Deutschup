## 8. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Grammar coverage is 57% on-time, 27% late, 7% missing.** The biggest issue is not WHAT DeutschUp teaches, but WHEN. Key A1 grammar (Akkusativ, Modalverben, Trennbare Verben) is delayed to A2, creating a foundation gap.

**Vocabulary is at 38%** of textbook density. Each lesson needs 3-4x more words.

**6 grammar topics are completely missing** from DeutschUp.

**Recommended approach:**
1. Restructure A1 grammar sequence to match textbook timing
2. Add 6 missing grammar topics
3. Expand vocabulary per lesson
4. Add exercise variety (reading, writing, discussion)
