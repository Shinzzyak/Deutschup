## 8. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**DeutschUp is structurally strong** — grammar progression from A1→B2 is comprehensive and matches/exceeds standard textbooks. The B2 content is unique and valuable.

**The critical gap is vocabulary depth and scenario variety.** DeutschUp covers ~750 words vs ~2,500 in standard textbooks. This is the single biggest area for improvement.

**Recommended priority:**
1. Expand vocabulary per lesson (target: 2,000+ words by end of A2)
2. Add 3 missing A1 grammar topics
3. Add scenario-based exercises (hotel, job interview, phone, email)
4. Add writing and reading comprehension exercises

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks for Tier 1 improvements.
